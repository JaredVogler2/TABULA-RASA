# 01-PRODUCT-AND-SCIENCE — Product vision and scientific charter

Read CLAUDE.md and prompts/00-MASTER.md. Implement this phase; record evidence, update progress, commit.

Design the full product and define falsifiable research questions before implementation. TABULA RASA is an observer/intervention strategy sandbox, not a conventional city builder or an LLM chat room. The fictional world begins with 50 unique adults, procedural geography, food, water, energy, shelter, finite inventory, rudimentary communication and physical interaction. Start with heterogeneity in traits, skills and needs; no assigned leaders, religions, money, businesses, occupations, laws, classes or scripted stages. Agents may propose such concepts; they are not guaranteed to emerge. Record all designer-supplied concepts, action affordances, prompt priors (including pretraining contamination), initial resources and environmental assumptions in a visible affordance registry. Distinguish spontaneous emergence within a designed model from human societal emergence; do not claim novel scientific discovery or consciousness.
Design two experiences: a visually engaging Observer mode (watch, inspect, pause, step, accelerate, intervene, rewind, fork, compare, choose challenges) and a Lab mode (controlled experiments, seeds, metrics, cohorts, cost and data export). A 5–15 minute first session should teach causality through actual decisions and visible consequences. Specify win-less sandbox plus optional objectives e.g. survive winter or reduce inequality without direct mind control. Define at least three scenarios with distinct conditions, no prescribed solution. Specify controls on desktop and iPhone, privacy, accessibility and non-exploitative design. Define the treatment groups, operational definitions, pre-registered hypotheses and known confounds. Read primary artificial-life, agent-based modeling, social simulation and institutional economics research; document citations with relevance, not invented results. 
Gate: product/design charter, experiment protocol, affordance register, initial scenarios, acceptance matrix and UI flow; identify unresolved decisions, prioritize a vertical slice.
