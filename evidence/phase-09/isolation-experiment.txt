Seed 5, nolock: nobody ever joined (0 members, 23 announcements, 2 signatures on proposals that lapsed),
yet 22 were alive against 35 with institutions off. Deaths cluster on days 21-26 in both arms.
Reading: outcomes near the survival margin are chaotic; a perturbation of a few dozen one-tick acts
moves the death toll by more than the six-seed mean difference between arms. No conclusion about
the effect of the control can be drawn from these runs.
