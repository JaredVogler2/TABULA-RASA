# A notice sat on top of the time controls on every phone

Seen in the release check's own still of the deployed site as a phone (evidence/phase-18/deployed-phone-2-world.jpg:
"Field journal · Something put by" over the step button and the clock), then measured.

On a screen 560 px wide or narrower the time bar is two rows tall — play, step and clock above the six speeds —
and a notice was still placed where it clears a bar of one row.

Measured in headless Chromium against the production build: a world is made, the game is saved from the menu,
and the notice ("Saved. Snapshot …") and the time bar are measured. Top and bottom edges in CSS pixels.
