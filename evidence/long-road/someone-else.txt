The fix (the test; nothing in the game). The view is widened a notch at a time — the picture let come to
rest after each — until someone else can be tapped, as far as the view goes (fourteen notches); if every view
is empty, a tick at a time is let pass, for at most half a day. The test then asserts that someone was there
to tap (and says the tick if not), that neither zooming out nor time passing let go of the person followed —
the button and the camera both — and everything it asserted before.
