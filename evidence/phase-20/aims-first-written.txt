Each row: the aim as it is now counted, and beside it what two of the aims asked for before they were reworded:
how many people were lost to cold or hunger before spring ("lose no one", The Long Winter), and the first morning
on which any grove or fishing water stood below its collapse threshold ("keep every commons above", Thin Commons).
Both are counted in every scenario, to show that neither could be met anywhere.
